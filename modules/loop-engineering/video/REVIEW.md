# Review: Loop engineering, "The overnight build"

## Creative calls

- **Storyline:** A, chosen by Mou. B (The test that lied) and C (The machine on the desk) are in `STORYLINES.md`.
- **Night to day:** the night is the Night desk look and the morning is the Paper look, so the film's one colour flood is also the move from dark theme to light.
- **Camera:** the loop is one frame that rebuilds. A camera frames whichever nodes are on screen and pulls back each time a node arrives (tests, checker, progress.md), so the diagram always fills the frame.
- **Timing:** the token counter and the clock share the empty top-left grid cell as story objects, not decoration.
- **Narration:** 276 words, Kokoro `af_heart` at 0.9 speed, 1:48 in all. Every cue is timed from `beats.json`, measured from the voice files.

## Measured and looked at

- `check`: passes. Layout, contrast, motion and audio show no findings.
- `snapshot --review`: beat, seam and phone sheets, plus the SPEC QA frames at 2.5, 9, 10.6, 14, 21, 30, 37, 46, 51.6, 58, 66, 77.5, 85, 92.5, 97 and 106 s.
- Fixed after review:
  - the loop sat small in the right half of the frame, so a camera now frames it
  - the edge labels on two-way edges overlapped, so the pairs were separated
  - two notes ran past their boxes
  - all text was raised to the 28px floor
  - the frame was empty at 0 s
  - there were dead stretches during b02–b04
  - the claim left "it." on a line of its own
- **Sound, measured but not heard:** the rendered MP4 is −14.3 LUFS integrated with a −2.1 dBTP true peak and no clipping. To get there, each voice clip was normalized with ffmpeg loudnorm (I −16, TP −1.5) and the voice lane plays at 0.9. Nobody has listened to it.

## Warnings kept on purpose

- `composition_file_too_large` (loop.html, ~430 lines): the loop is one continuous scene by design, and it is generated by `tools/build.mjs`.
- `text_below_floor` on the terminal title bar: it only triggers during the 0.55 s shrink into the You node.

## Weakest shot and next steps

- **Weakest shot:** the morning. The result panel and the claim sit on a large white field with little motion. With more time, the morning would reuse the loop diagram in Paper colours, all green, before the claim.
- **No music or sound effects:** the music catalog needs a HeyGen sign-in (`! npx hyperframes auth login`), so the film is voice only.

## Delivered

`renders/loop-engineering.mp4`: 1920×1080, 30 fps, 1:48, 6.3 MB, delivery quality.

## Rebuilding

The compositions are generated. After editing `narration.json`, regenerate the voice with `HYPERFRAMES_PYTHON=~/.cache/hyperframes-kokoro/bin/python` and `npx hyperframes tts`, normalize each clip with `ffmpeg -i bNN.wav -af loudnorm=I=-16:TP=-1.5:LRA=11 -ar 24000`, rebuild `beats.json`, then run `node tools/build.mjs`. Hand edits made in the HyperFrames app are overwritten on the next build.
